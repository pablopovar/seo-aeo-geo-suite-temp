METHODOLOGY

Scope, limits, and sources

  • This run evaluates the submitted page's fetched HTML plus response headers, robots.txt, and one sitemap candidate.
  • It does not execute JavaScript, crawl the whole site, query Search Console, test live AI answers, validate business claims, or measure rankings.
  • Thresholds for lengths and counts are review heuristics, not universal ranking factors.
  • GEO and AEO overlap with foundational SEO, accessibility, structured content, evidence quality, and information architecture.
  • All summaries are calculated from the same 116 finding records shown in this report.
